# Basic IELTS Reading Unit 12

Imported from the supplied `Unit 12 Vocabulary - Verified.pptx`. The title slide
is excluded. All 35 vocabulary slides retain their original order, terms,
parts of speech, definitions and examples: 19 from Help! Volunteers Wanted and
16 from Modern Examinations.

Each card uses the picture from its matching slide, including its slide crop.
Pictures are encoded as WebP within 680 by 360 pixels. `provenance.json` records
the slide number, passage, original word fields and picture checksums.
Original PowerPoint bytes are retained in the supplied upload, not duplicated
in this repository.

Cloze answers use the actual form found in each example, including abridged,
appreciated and interviews. Space-separated forms of drop-in, self-access and
sub-part are also accepted. Every completed cloze restores its source example.

Reimport vocabulary with:

```sh
python3 -B scripts/import_unit_12.py /absolute/path/to/source.pptx
```

The importer preserves other units and any existing Unit 12 reading reference.
Run the Pages build afterwards to update the generated asset hashes.

Original reading pages and reviewed answers are recorded with the other Basic
IELTS Reading units under `source-assets/basic-ielts-reading/`. Unit 12 includes
both passages, 39 numbered questions and nine book pages, 99–107. The supplied
scan omits its listed publisher answer-key section, so WordHop prepared keys
and English explanations from the reading text.

Passage 1 Question 5 is shown with an explanation but not scored: the printed
choices compare importance, whereas sub-part means a subdivision. Question 2
accepts both A and B because its printed definitions only approximate the
meaning of assigned in context.
